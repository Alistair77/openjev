# Question design

Make options mutually distinguishable. Describe what each covers, what it excludes when options overlap, and examples only when they disambiguate a real ambiguity. Avoid labels such as `low`, `medium`, and `high` without concrete level descriptions.

For complex decisions, ask separate questions and combine them in application logic. For example, score market size, feasibility, and differentiation separately instead of asking for one broad investment judgment.
